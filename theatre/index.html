Voices & delivery

Neural mode downloads model files and code from Hugging Face and jsDelivr and uses substantial RAM. Loading starts only on Load, Play or Preview. It never silently switches to a device voice. Play prepares neural dialogue before starting the scene. Generation runs in your browser.

Speed and tone apply to the next playback. Tone gently changes equalization, not emotion or vocal identity. Voice previews read your first line for that character. Choices stay in this browser.
